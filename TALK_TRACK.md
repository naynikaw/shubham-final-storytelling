# The Geography of Intelligence: 2-Minute Talk Track

**Tone:** Natural, curious, conversational.
**Total Time:** ~2 Minutes

---

### 0:00 - The Hook (Hero & Intro)
*(Start at top)*

"Hi everyone. We all know the narrative: AI is decentralized. Thanks to the cloud and remote work, the next unicorn can be built anywhere."

*(Scroll to **The Signal in the Noise**)*

"I wanted to believe that. But when I tracked 940 venture-backed AI startups from the last year, the data told a different story. It’s not a story about a flat world. It’s a story about a fortress."

### 0:30 - The Source & Location (Universities & Geography)
*(Scroll to **The Academic Pipeline**, then **Geographic Concentration**)*

"It starts here. The 'garage startup' is dead. This wave is coming directly from the labs of Stanford, MIT, and Berkeley. These schools are the supply chain.

And when those founders leave the lab, they don't disperse. They cluster.
Almost a third of the entire global AI industry is packed into a 30-mile radius in the Bay Area. Despite remote work, proximity still wins. If you're outside this circle, you're playing on hard mode."

### 1:00 - The Scale (Global Capital)
*(Scroll to **Global Capital Dominance**)*

"Globally, it’s even starker. The US captured 76% of all funding. The gap between the US and the UK isn't a gap; it's a chasm. Structural advantages have turned AI into a massive American export."

### 1:30 - The Shift & Takeaway (Trends & Playbook)
*(Scroll to **Temporal Trends**, then **The Founder's Playbook**)*

"But the 'easy money' phase is cooling off. Capital is down. The builders are moving from 'Bits' to 'Atoms'—robotics and machinery.

So, for founders, the map is clear:
1.  **Scout the Labs:** Talent is academic.
2.  **Get to the Bay:** Serendipity is local.
3.  **Build Real Things:** Solve physical problems.

The hype is over. The building has begun."

*(End)*
